② sign in (one-click SIWE)

Generates a fresh keypair, mints a nonce, signs the SIWE message, and posts to /verify/ — all in one click. Captures the JWT for subsequent steps.

— 
—

③ submit a domain

Creates a Domain in pending status. Use the dev shortcut to skip DNS verification and jump straight to vaulted — saves you publishing a real TXT record to exercise the rest of the flow.

— 
—

④ create a campaign

Once the domain is vaulted, attach a ShardCampaign. Creating it transitions the domain to sharding; activating it transitions to active and opens it for purchases.

— 
—

⑤ buy shards (as curator)

Buys shards as the same authenticated wallet. Production-wise a curator would be a different wallet from the founder, but the schema doesn't prevent founders from buying their own campaign — see the v0.4.0 commit notes.
